# Hydraulic model guide

EPANET is the intended hydraulic engine; WNTR may support documented resilience and controlled simulation workflows. Neither is integrated in this milestone. A network model must come from an authoritative or explicitly benchmark source and must retain boundary conditions, units, demand patterns, roughness assumptions, calibration evidence, and version.

Before accepting results: confirm model syntax and connectivity, establish units and time-step semantics, record solver warnings/errors, compare expected benchmark outputs, and label every output SIMULATED. Do not treat simulated pressures or flows as field observations. Use a research namespace for public benchmark networks and scenarios.
